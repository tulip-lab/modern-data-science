# Welcome to Modern Data Science

Modern Data Science combines data acquisition, data preparation, scalable processing, analytics, evaluation, privacy, and communication. This common core introduces those areas through a sequence of modules that can support different universities and teaching periods.

In the era of large language models and agentic AI, data science capability remains essential. Automated tools can help draft code, explore ideas, and accelerate parts of a workflow, but trustworthy results still depend on people who understand data quality, sampling, bias, modelling assumptions, evaluation, privacy, provenance, and domain context.

## Assumed Knowledge

The common core is designed for advanced undergraduate and postgraduate learners. Prior programming experience is helpful but not required. Learners who are new to Python should begin with [Python Foundations](../M02-Python/README.md).

## Learning Approach

You will be asked to:

- read and question the module explanations and references;
- run or adapt practical examples where available;
- document data sources, assumptions, decisions, and limitations;
- check results rather than relying on tool output alone;
- communicate findings in a reproducible and responsible way.

The modules may be used for supported classes or self-directed study. Your university offering determines the actual schedule, required activities, assessment, collaboration rules, and policy requirements.
