# USTB 2026 Assignment: Group Forecasting Project

## Overview

This assignment uses the public SIT742 2026 Assignment 2 forecasting project materials. Students complete a reproducible group forecasting workflow using the public tourism-demand dataset and submit the required outputs according to the USTB-specific requirements below.

Unless otherwise stated on this page, follow the public SIT742 2026 Assignment 2 materials.

## Submission Files

Submit the following files through the USTB-specified platform:

```text
USTB-2026-<GroupID>.ipynb
USTB-2026-<GroupID>.pdf
USTB-2026-<GroupID>-Forecast.csv
USTB-2026-<GroupID>-Video.<approved format or link>
```

Adjust the filename pattern if the USTB platform requires a different convention.

You should receive an email acknowledgement from Prof. Gang Li within 48 hours after successful submission. If you do not receive an acknowledgement within 48 hours, contact Prof. Li and resend the submission if needed. Use a reliable email account where possible; avoid sending from `qq.com` or other mail services that may be delayed or filtered in international email delivery.
