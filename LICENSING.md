## Datasets and Third-Party Resources

Datasets, images, handouts, recordings, model files, archives, extracts, linked resources, and other third-party materials may have their own licences, terms, attribution requirements, access controls, or use restrictions. Their presence or linkage here does not mean that repository contributors can relicense them.

Before redistributing or adapting third-party material:

1. identify the original source and rights holder;
2. verify the applicable licence or permission;
3. retain required attribution and notices;
4. check privacy, accessibility, platform, research-use, and institutional restrictions;
5. record unresolved cases instead of assuming that the repository licences apply.
